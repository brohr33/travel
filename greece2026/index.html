Flight Choice

Option A: Prague

Recommended if timing is Jul 17

  • Outbound award: CVG-PRG departs Jul 16 at 6:00 AM; 42.5k Capital One points + CAD 143 economy, or seats from 75k + CAD 143 with 92% in business class.
  • ATH connection: SKY express PRG-ATH, 10:50 AM-2:20 PM, 2h 30m, nonstop, USD 168 shown. This becomes same-day cruise arrival if you overnight in Prague on Jul 17.
  • Hotel target: Hotel Clement Prague, 4-star, central but quieter Old Town/Petrska area; official rates shown from about EUR 50-95 depending room type/date.
  • Evening idea: Jazz Boat or Crystal Dinner Vltava cruise for lit-up Prague views with dinner/live music.

Option B: Budapest

Recommended for lower cash flight

  • Outbound award: 75k Capital One points + CAD 143.
  • ATH connection: Aegean BUD-ATH, 6:55 PM-9:50 PM, 1h 55m, nonstop, USD 101 shown.
  • Hotel target: Prestige Hotel Budapest, 4-star superior, central near Danube, Chain Bridge, St. Stephen's Basilica and Andrassy Avenue.
  • Evening idea: Legenda Danube Legend one-hour evening cruise, then dinner near the Basilica or Danube promenade.

Barcelona Day Plan

Hotel Area

  • Best fit: Gothic Quarter / El Born / Placa de Catalunya area for a short stay.
  • Practical hotel candidate: Hotel Barcelona Catedral, central Gothic Quarter, close to Cathedral, Las Ramblas, Placa Catalunya, Picasso Museum and Palau de la Musica.
  • Port transfer: taxi from cruise terminal to Gothic Quarter is the simplest option with luggage.

Day Structure

  • Morning: disembark, taxi to hotel, drop bags.
  • Late morning/afternoon: Sagrada Familia timed entry, then Passeig de Gracia or Gothic Quarter walk.
  • Evening: tapas dinner in Gothic Quarter or El Born.
  • Jul 26: depart BCN 3:15 PM on Delta itinerary; plan airport arrival around 12:15 PM.

Barcelona 2026 demand may be elevated around Gaudi-related travel. Use official ticket channels where possible and book Sagrada Familia/Park Guell timed entries once flights are final.
